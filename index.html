Some dates are just dates. And then there's 20 July 2026 — the day you came into my life. And as if the universe wanted to make it unforgettable, it was your birthday too. One day, double the magic: the day you were born, and the day we began.

People search their whole lives for a person who feels like home. I found mine on the 20th of July.
